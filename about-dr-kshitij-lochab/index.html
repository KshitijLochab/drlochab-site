About

Dr. Kshitij Lochab

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 18 October 2026

Consultant Gastroenterologist & Therapeutic Endoscopist, Gurgaon (Gurugram)

I treat diseases of the food pipe, stomach, intestines, liver, gallbladder and pancreas. My special training is in advanced endoscopy, including endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) and ERCP, which lets many problems that once needed surgery be treated through a scope. I take time to explain your condition and your options, so that every decision is one we make together.
