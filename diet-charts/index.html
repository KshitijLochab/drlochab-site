Diet charts

Indian diet charts for gut and liver health

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 19 October 2026

Share on WhatsApp

Patients often ask what exactly they should eat. These charts turn the advice into a normal Indian day, with veg and non-veg options, a few Western choices, and caveats for diabetes, high BP, kidney disease, pregnancy and older age. The food diary shows what your day has so far, what is missing, and what to eat for the rest of the day.
