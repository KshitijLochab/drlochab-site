Log a few things each day: bowel habits, symptoms, possible triggers, water and activity. The weekly graphs show patterns, and the report gives your doctor a clear picture in two minutes.

Your data stays on your phone

  • Entries are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to Dr. Lochab, to this website or to anyone else.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking. This page is technically blocked from sending data anywhere.
  • You can lock your entries with a PIN, which encrypts them on this phone.
  • Your data leaves this phone only if you print it, download it or share it yourself.

Your browser is blocking storage, so entries cannot be saved here. Try a normal (not private) window.

Summary for your doctor

Common questions

Can Dr. Lochab or anyone else see my entries?

No. Entries are stored only in the browser on your phone. They are never uploaded, and the page is blocked from sending data. Only you can print or share them.

What happens if I change phones or clear my browser?

Clearing browser data deletes the entries. Use 'Download a backup' to save a file, and 'Restore from a backup' on the new phone.

How long should I track before seeing a doctor?

Two to four weeks of entries give a useful picture. Do not wait to track if you have warning signs such as blood in the stool, black stools, weight loss or vomiting.
