What are the warning signs of stomach cancer?

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 10 October 2026

Short answer

Most indigestion and acidity is not cancer. But some symptoms, especially when they are new after age 45 to 50 or keep getting worse, need an endoscopy: weight loss without trying, difficulty swallowing, repeated vomiting, black stools, anaemia and feeling full after a few bites. Stomach cancer found early can often be cured.

Warning signs

These can also be caused by ulcers and other treatable problems, which is another reason to get checked.

  • Indigestion or upper belly pain that is new and persistent, especially after age 45 to 50
  • Weight loss or loss of appetite without trying
  • Difficulty swallowing, or food sticking
  • Repeated vomiting, or vomiting blood
  • Black, tarry stools, or iron-deficiency anaemia
  • Feeling full after only a few bites

Risk factors

  • Long-term H. pylori infection
  • Smoking and alcohol
  • A diet high in salted, pickled or smoked foods, and low in fruits and vegetables
  • A family history of stomach cancer
  • Some long-standing stomach conditions found on endoscopy

How it is checked

An upper GI endoscopy is the test. It looks directly at the stomach and allows biopsies to be taken painlessly. Scans such as CT and EUS are used afterwards to find out how far a cancer has spread, if one is found.

Lowering the risk

  • Test for and treat H. pylori when advised
  • Stop smoking and cut down alcohol
  • Eat more fruits and vegetables, and less salted and pickled food
  • Do not keep taking acidity tablets for months without finding the cause
