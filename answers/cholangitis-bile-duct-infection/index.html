What is cholangitis (bile duct infection)?

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 10 October 2026

Share on WhatsApp

Short answer

Cholangitis is an infection of the bile duct, almost always because the duct is blocked by a stone, a narrowing, a growth or a blocked stent. It causes fever with chills, jaundice and pain in the upper right belly. It is an emergency: it needs antibiotics and urgent drainage of the bile duct, usually by ERCP.

Symptoms

  • Fever with shivering
  • Yellow eyes and skin, dark urine
  • Pain in the upper right belly
  • In severe cases: confusion, low blood pressure and drowsiness

Common causes

  • Stones that have slipped from the gallbladder into the bile duct
  • A narrowing (stricture) of the bile duct
  • Cancer of the pancreas or bile duct
  • A bile duct stent that has become blocked, which is why stent change dates should never be missed

Treatment

  • Antibiotics through a drip and fluids
  • Urgent drainage of the bile duct by ERCP, removing the stone or placing a stent, usually within 24 to 48 hours and sooner if the person is very unwell
  • If ERCP is not possible, drainage through EUS or through the skin by a radiologist
  • Later, removal of the gallbladder if stones were the cause

When to go to hospital

Fever with chills and yellow eyes, especially in someone with gallstones or a bile duct stent, needs the emergency department the same day. Call 112 if there is confusion or fainting.

Diet after ERCP and recovery

Eat more

  • After ERCP: start with sips of water, then light food such as khichdi, dal-rice and curd, as advised
  • Small, low-fat meals until the gallbladder is removed, if stones were the cause
  • Plenty of fluids during recovery

Cut down

  • Heavy, fried or very fatty meals in the first few weeks
  • Alcohol
  • Eating if there is severe belly pain or vomiting after ERCP; contact your doctor

Good to know

  • Keep stent change dates. A blocked stent is a common cause of repeat cholangitis.
  • Fever with chills after a stent needs the same-day attention.

Common questions

Is ERCP painful?

It is done under sedation or anaesthesia, so most people do not remember it. Some have a sore throat or bloating for a day.

How long can a bile duct stent stay?

Plastic stents are usually changed or removed within about three months. Metal stents stay longer. Your doctor will give you a date.

Can cholangitis come back?

Yes, if the cause remains, such as gallstones or a narrowing. Removing the gallbladder and treating the narrowing prevent most repeat episodes.
