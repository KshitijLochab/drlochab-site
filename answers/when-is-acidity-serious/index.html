When is acidity a sign of something serious?

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 10 October 2026

Share on WhatsApp

Short answer

Most acidity is simple reflux that settles with habit changes and short courses of medicine. See a doctor if it happens twice a week or more, lasts beyond 2 to 4 weeks, or comes with any warning sign.

Warning signs

  • Difficulty or pain while swallowing
  • Weight loss without trying
  • Vomiting blood or black, tar-like stools
  • Anaemia or unusual tiredness
  • Persistent vomiting
  • New symptoms starting later in life

Rule out the heart first

Chest pain with sweating, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw can be a heart attack, even when it feels like acidity. Go to an emergency department straight away.

What a gastroenterologist checks

An upper GI endoscopy shows inflammation, ulcers, a hiatus hernia or Barrett's oesophagus, and tests for H. pylori. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

Simple steps that help

  • Dinner at least 3 hours before bed
  • Smaller meals
  • Raising the head end of the bed
  • Losing extra weight
  • Less tea, coffee, fried food, smoking and alcohol
