When is acidity a sign of something serious?

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Medically reviewed by Dr. Kshitij Lochab on 31 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

Share on WhatsApp

Short answer

Most acidity is simple reflux that settles with habit changes and short courses of medicine. See a doctor if it happens twice a week or more, lasts beyond 2 to 4 weeks, or comes with any warning sign.

Warning signs

  • Difficulty or pain while swallowing
  • Weight loss without trying
  • Vomiting blood or black, tar-like stools
  • Anaemia or unusual tiredness
  • Persistent vomiting
  • New symptoms starting later in life

Rule out the heart first

Chest pain with sweating, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw can be a heart attack, even when it feels like acidity. Go to an emergency department straight away.

What a gastroenterologist checks

An upper GI endoscopy shows inflammation, ulcers, a hiatus hernia or Barrett's oesophagus, and tests for H. pylori. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

Simple steps that help

  • Dinner at least 3 hours before bed
  • Smaller meals
  • Raising the head end of the bed
  • Losing extra weight
  • Less tea, coffee, fried food, smoking and alcohol

Daily habits that keep acidity in check

Eat more

  • A proper breakfast, rather than only tea or coffee on an empty stomach, if that brings on burning
  • Sleeping on the left side if acidity troubles you at night
  • Loose clothes around the waist, and a short walk after meals

Cut down

  • Bending, lifting or heavy exercise soon after a meal
  • Very spicy, sour or oily food, chocolate, mint and fizzy drinks, if they set off your symptoms
  • Painkillers for body pain taken often without a doctor's advice, as they can cause ulcers

Good to know

  • Triggers differ from person to person. Avoid what bothers you, not every food on a list.
  • Buying acidity tablets over the counter for weeks can hide a problem. If you need them often, see a doctor.

Common questions

Can acidity cause chest pain or breathlessness?

Reflux can cause a burning pain behind the breastbone, and sometimes a cough or wheeze at night. But chest pain and breathlessness can also come from the heart or lungs. Chest pain with sweating, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the arm, jaw or back is an emergency: call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Can long-standing acidity turn into cancer?

Rarely. In a small number of people, years of reflux change the lining of the food pipe, called Barrett's oesophagus, which carries a slightly higher risk of cancer. This is why reflux that has lasted for years, or comes with warning signs, is checked with an endoscopy.

Why does my acidity keep coming back?

Reflux is often a long-term tendency, linked to weight, meal timing, smoking and a weak valve at the top of the stomach. Medicines settle the symptoms but do not change these, so the daily habits matter. If it keeps returning, an endoscopy helps find the reason.
