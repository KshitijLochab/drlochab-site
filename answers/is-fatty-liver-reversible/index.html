Is fatty liver reversible?

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Medically reviewed by Dr. Kshitij Lochab on 31 October 2026

Share on WhatsApp

Short answer

Yes. In its early stages, fatty liver can be fully reversed. Losing 7 to 10% of your body weight, being active every day and cutting sugar, refined flour and alcohol can clear the extra fat and even reverse early scarring.

How much weight do I need to lose?

Studies show that losing about 5% of body weight reduces liver fat, 7% can calm the inflammation, and 10% can reverse early scarring. For someone who weighs 80 kg, that means 4 to 8 kg.

Aim for half to one kilo a week. Very fast weight loss can worsen the liver and cause gallstones.

What helps the most

  • Cutting sugary drinks, sweets and packaged juices
  • Replacing maida with whole grains, millets and dal
  • Vegetables or salad with every meal
  • At least 150 minutes of brisk walking a week, which helps even before the weight falls
  • Avoiding alcohol completely

When it may not fully reverse

Once fatty liver has progressed to heavy scarring (cirrhosis), the scarring usually does not go away completely. Even then, the same steps slow the damage and protect the liver. This is why finding it early matters.

How do I know if my liver is scarred?

Fatty liver usually causes no symptoms, so tests are needed. Liver blood tests, an ultrasound and a liver stiffness test (elastography) show how much fat and scarring there is, and guide how often you need follow-up.

Everyday swaps that take fat off the liver

Eat more

  • Protein at every meal, such as dal, chana, curd, paneer, eggs or fish, which keeps you full for longer
  • Food roasted, steamed or made in a tandoor instead of fried, with cooking oil measured by the spoon
  • Plain water, chaas, lemon water without sugar, and tea with little or no sugar

Cut down

  • Namkeen, bhujia, biscuits and other packaged snacks between meals
  • Packaged fruit juices and smoothies, even those labelled 'no added sugar'
  • Late, heavy dinners and second helpings at night

Good to know

  • Weight lost slowly and kept off matters more than any one diet. Choose changes you can keep for years.
  • Muscle work, such as squats, climbing stairs or light weights two or three days a week, helps the liver even when the weight changes little.

Common questions

How long does it take to reverse fatty liver?

It depends on where you start. Liver fat often begins to fall within a few months of steady weight loss and daily activity. Scarring takes longer to improve, often a year or more. Repeat tests show the progress.

Will fatty liver come back if I put the weight back on?

Yes, it can. The fat returns when the weight, sugar or alcohol returns. This is why small changes you can keep up work better than a strict diet for a few weeks.

Can fatty liver improve without losing weight?

Partly. Regular exercise, cutting sugar and stopping alcohol help the liver even before the weight falls. For most people who are overweight, losing some weight gives the biggest benefit. People of normal weight with fatty liver also improve with exercise and a better diet.

Do detox drinks or home remedies clear liver fat?

No. There is no proof that detox drinks, juices or liver tonics remove liver fat, and some herbal products can harm the liver. Ask your doctor before taking any.
