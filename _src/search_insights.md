# What people search before they reach drlochab.com

From Google Search Console exports sent by Dr. Lochab. Use this when choosing the weekly new answer page, wording titles and
writing common questions. Add a dated section for each new export; keep older ones for comparison.

## 14 Oct to 10 Nov 2026 (28 days, web search)
**Totals:** 21 clicks, 2,166 impressions, about 1% click rate. Impressions per week: 155, 604, 611, 796 (rising).
India: 20 clicks, 1,661 impressions. Phones: 17 clicks, 1,530 impressions, average position 9. 139 of the 218 pages were shown at least once.

### What this says
1. **Almost nobody arrives yet.** About 5 visitors a week from Google. 13 of the 21 clicks went to the home page, mostly from people searching his name.
2. **His own name is not yet in first place.** "dr kshitij lochab": 22 impressions, 3 clicks, average position 3.9 (hospital and directory listings sit above).
3. **Searches that mean "I want a doctor" barely show the site.** "gastroenterologist gurgaon" position 82, "gastroenterologist near me" 72, "fatty liver treatment in gurgaon" 89, "ercp treatment in gurgaon" 67. Closest: "endoscopic ultrasound in gurgaon" (9 impressions, position 24) and "eus in gurgaon" (position 20). These are won by Google Business Profile, reviews and directory listings more than by pages.
4. **The demand that does find the site is Hindi, typed in Roman letters.** 210 of 370 visible searches are Hinglish. Hindi pages: 1,278 impressions; English pages: 996. Answer pages: 1,418 impressions; condition pages: 24 in total (10 of the 14 English condition pages were never shown).
5. **Biggest cluster: what to eat in jaundice** (161 different searches, 287 impressions, 0 clicks). `/hi/answers/diet-in-jaundice/` had 358 impressions at average position 34. People ask food by food: poha (position 6 to 10 already), aalu, dahi, seb, rice, roti, chana, paneer, dry fruits, meetha, namak, chai, coffee, which sabzi, which fruit; also "पीलिया में परहेज" (position 80), "पीलिया में टहलना चाहिए" (10), "पीलिया का इलाज" (61).
6. **Best-performing page:** `/hi/answers/tb-medicines-and-liver/`: 351 impressions, position 6.4, 4 clicks. Main search: "tb ki dawa se liver kharab hota hai".
7. **"Is X good for Y" questions land on diet charts:** "is idli good for pancreatitis" (position 9.5), "is dalia good for gastritis" (10.5), "gluten free indian diet" (6.8), "celiac disease diet chart" (30).

Other clusters: worms ("pet mein keede symptoms" position 5, "pet ke kide in english", "pet me kide ki dawa"); burping with hiccups (mostly from outside India); liver abscess (position 50 to 80); SGPT/SGOT in Hindi (position 50 to 95: "sgot test kya hai", "sgpt कितना होना चाहिए"); typhoid ("typhoid bukhar ke lakshan" 60); stool test ("stool test kya hota hai" 48); black vomit ("kali ulti hona" 13); medicine-name searches ("providac capsule uses in hindi", "wonderlac pro", "ibspro", "coloprep"): do not write brand pages (NMC).
