## Voice
Plain, warm, short sentences. Everyday Indian foods and routines. One practical action per post. Hindi in simple spoken style, keeping common words like एसिडिटी, फैटी लिवर, एंडोस्कोपी.

## Rules (NMC and platforms)
- Education only: no testimonials, before/after photos, "best"/"No. 1" claims, success percentages, discounts or paid ads asking people to book.
- No patient photos, endoscopy images or case details without written consent and full de-identification.
- No medicine doses or brand names. Conservative, guideline-consistent medical content.
- Google Business Profile posts: no phone numbers in the text (Google rejects them); use the Call or Learn more button. Image 1200x900.
- Instagram: 1080x1350; carousels of 4-6 slides; 5-8 relevant hashtags.
- WhatsApp Status: 1080x1920. Broadcasts only to patients who opted in, at most weekly.
- Always include an emergency line when discussing warning signs: call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.
