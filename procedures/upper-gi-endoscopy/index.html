Gastroscopy

Upper GI endoscopy

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 8 October 2026

A thin camera passed through the mouth to look at the food pipe, stomach and first part of the intestine. Small painless biopsies can be taken.

What it is used for

  • Acidity or upper belly pain that does not settle
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Vomiting blood or black stools
  • Unexplained anaemia or weight loss
  • Testing for H. pylori and celiac disease
  • Checking for varices in liver disease

What to expect

Preparation
Nothing to eat for 6 to 8 hours
Time
About 10 to 15 minutes
Comfort
Throat spray, or light sedation if you prefer
After
Home the same day

Risks

Endoscopy is very safe. A mild sore throat for a day is common. Bleeding or a tear is rare, mostly when treatment is done during the test.

Conditions it is used for

Acidity & reflux (GERD) →H. pylori, gastritis & ulcers →Swallowing difficulty →Cirrhosis →

Common questions

Can I eat after an endoscopy?

Yes, once the throat numbness wears off, usually within an hour. Start with something light.

Will I be awake?

You can choose throat spray only, or light sedation so you are drowsy and relaxed. Most people remember very little.
