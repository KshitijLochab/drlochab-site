Variceal banding

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 10 October 2026

In liver disease, veins in the food pipe can swell and bleed. Small rubber bands placed through the scope shrink them and prevent or stop bleeding.

What it is used for

  • Preventing a first bleed from varices in cirrhosis
  • Stopping active bleeding from varices
  • Preventing repeat bleeding

What to expect

Preparation
Nothing to eat for 6 to 8 hours
Time
About 15 to 20 minutes
Comfort
Done under sedation
After
Soft diet for a day or two; repeat sessions may be needed

Risks

Chest discomfort and mild pain on swallowing for a day or two are common. Bleeding from the small ulcer left after banding is uncommon.

Conditions it is used for

Cirrhosis →

Common questions

How many sessions are needed?

Usually several, 2 to 4 weeks apart, until the veins are gone. Checks continue afterwards.

Will I also need medicines?

Often yes. Medicines that lower pressure in the liver veins (beta-blockers) are commonly used with banding.
