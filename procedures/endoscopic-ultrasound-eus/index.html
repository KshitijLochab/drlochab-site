EUS

Endoscopic ultrasound

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 8 October 2026

An ultrasound probe on the tip of the scope gives close images of the pancreas, bile duct and nearby glands from inside the body. It can take needle samples and drain fluid collections.

What it is used for

  • Pancreatic cysts and lumps
  • Bile duct stones that scans miss
  • Staging growths of the food pipe, stomach and pancreas
  • Needle samples (biopsies) from lumps and lymph nodes
  • Draining fluid collections after pancreatitis

What to expect

Preparation
Nothing to eat for 6 to 8 hours
Time
About 20 to 60 minutes
Comfort
Done under sedation
Used for
Pancreas cysts and lumps, bile duct problems, staging of growths

Risks

EUS is very safe. When a needle sample or drainage is done, there is a small risk of bleeding, infection or pancreatitis.

Common questions

How is EUS different from a normal endoscopy?

A normal endoscopy shows the inner lining. EUS adds ultrasound, which sees through the wall to organs and lumps next to it.

When will biopsy results come?

Usually within a week. Some special tests take longer.
