Bile & pancreatic ducts

ERCP

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 8 October 2026

A scope with X-ray guidance reaches the opening of the bile and pancreatic ducts to remove stones and place stents through blockages, without any cuts.

What it is used for

  • Stones stuck in the bile duct
  • Blockage of the bile duct by a narrowing or growth (stenting)
  • Bile leaks after surgery
  • Stones and narrowings in the pancreatic duct

What to expect

Preparation
Fasting and blood tests beforehand
Time
About 30 to 60 minutes
Comfort
Done under deep sedation or anaesthesia
After
Often a short hospital stay

Risks

Pancreatitis is the most common complication, in a few out of every hundred people. Bleeding, infection and perforation are uncommon. These are explained before the procedure.

Conditions it is used for

Gallstones & bile duct stones →Jaundice →Pancreatitis →

Common questions

Will I need my gallbladder removed after ERCP?

Often yes. If stones came from the gallbladder, removing it prevents them coming back.

Are bile duct stents permanent?

Plastic stents are temporary and are usually changed or removed within a few months. Metal stents may stay longer.
