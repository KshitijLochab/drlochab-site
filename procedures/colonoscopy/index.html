Large bowel

Colonoscopy

By Dr. Kshitij Lochab, Consultant Gastroenterologist · Updated 8 October 2026

A camera passed through the back passage to examine the large bowel. Polyps found during the test are removed at the same time.

What it is used for

  • Blood in the stool
  • Change in bowel habit lasting weeks
  • Unexplained anaemia or weight loss
  • Checking and following up IBD
  • Screening for polyps and colon cancer

What to expect

Preparation
Light diet and a bowel-cleansing drink the day before
Time
About 20 to 45 minutes
Comfort
Usually done under sedation
After
Home the same day; do not drive that day

Risks

Bloating and cramps for a few hours are common. Bleeding after polyp removal or a tear in the bowel are rare.

Conditions it is used for

Colon polyps & colon cancer →Ulcerative colitis & Crohn's disease →Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) →Constipation →

Common questions

Is the bowel preparation difficult?

The cleansing drink is the hardest part, but it is important: a clean bowel lets small polyps be seen. Follow the timing exactly.

Will I feel pain?

Usually not. It is done under sedation, and you may feel some pressure or wind afterwards.
