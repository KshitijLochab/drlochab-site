When to see a gastroenterologist

  • Acidity or indigestion that keeps coming back, or needs tablets every day
  • Difficulty swallowing, vomiting or weight loss without trying
  • Blood in the stool, black stools or a change in bowel habit lasting more than three weeks
  • Yellow eyes, raised liver enzymes or fatty liver with diabetes
  • Long-lasting loose motions, constipation, bloating or belly pain
  • Gallstones, pancreatitis or a scan finding that needs explaining

Vomiting blood, black and tarry stools, severe belly pain or confusion in liver disease are emergencies: call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Before your visit

  • Bring all previous reports, scans, endoscopy and biopsy reports
  • Bring a list of every medicine and supplement you take
  • No fasting is needed for a consultation
